Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform

How do I take a Test or Exam?

To begin an assessment, navigate to either the "Test Library" or "Exam Library" from the side menu. Browse the list of available subjects and click on the one you wish to take. The test will start immediately after a brief instructions screen.

Make sure you have a stable internet connection before starting, and allow sufficient time to complete the assessment without interruptions.

Where can I see my results?

After you submit a test or exam, you will be shown a detailed results page with your score, correct answers, and areas for improvement. You can download a PDF certificate of this result for your records.

Past results are available on your Dashboard under the "Results History" section. Teachers and administrators have access to more detailed analytics and class performance reports.

Can I change my answer after selecting it?

Yes. As long as you have not submitted the exam, you can navigate back to any question using the navigation panel at the bottom of the screen and change your selected option.

The system automatically saves your answers as you progress through the test. You can also flag questions for review and return to them later before final submission.

What is the difference between a Test and an Exam?

In our system, "Tests" are typically shorter assessments or practice quizzes that may not contribute to your final grade. They are designed for self-assessment and practice.

"Exams" are formal, proctored assessments that contribute to your final grade. They often have time limits, stricter security measures, and are typically administered on a specific schedule.

What happens if I lose internet connection during a test?

Our system has an auto-save feature that preserves your answers every 30 seconds. If you lose connection, simply reconnect and you'll be able to resume where you left off.

For longer disruptions, the test will automatically pause and you'll have the ability to continue once you're back online. Your teacher or administrator can also assist with any technical issues.
