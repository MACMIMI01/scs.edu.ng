Guide & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I take a Test or Exam?

To begin an assessment, navigate to either the "Test Library" or "Exam Library" from the side menu. Browse the list of available subjects and click on the one you wish to take. The test will start immediately.

Where can I see my results?

After you submit a test or exam, you will be shown a results page. You can download a PDF of this result. Past results may be available on your Dashboard, depending on your user role.

Can I change my answer after selecting it?

Yes. As long as you have not submitted the exam, you can navigate back to any question using the number panel at the bottom of the screen and change your selected option.

What is the difference between a Test and an Exam?

In our system, "Tests" are typically shorter assessments or practice quizzes, while "Exams" are formal, term-end assessments that contribute to your final grade.

Getting Started Guide

  1. Log In or Register: Access the platform using your official school credentials. If you are a new user, you can register for temporary 60-minute access.
  2. Navigate the Dashboard: Your dashboard is your central hub. From here, you can access all major sections of the platform.
  3. Select an Assessment: Go to the "Test Library" or "Exam Library" to find your desired subject. You can use the search bar to filter the list quickly.
  4. Take the Test: Answer each question carefully. Use the navigation panel to jump between questions if needed. Your progress is saved as you move to the next question.
  5. Submit and View Results: Once you are finished, click the "Submit Exam" button. Your results will be calculated and displayed instantly. Don't forget to download your PDF result slip!
